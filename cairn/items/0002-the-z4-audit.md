## Ships

Fast exact Lee distance for Z4 codes at the lengths of the Aydin–Asamov
database, and a published, independent audit of that database: every entry
recomputed, certified and marked verified, open or discrepancy. Python bindings.

## Done when

- [ ] The fast engine agrees with brute force on the corpus and on 10,000 seeded random codes.
- [ ] Every database entry in scope has a ledger record with a status and, where verified, a certificate.
- [ ] Every discrepancy was reported to the maintainers before the audit was published.

## Explicitly not in this milestone

- GF(4) and quantum codes. Canonical forms. Search of any kind.
