## Problem

Deferred: upper bounds stay `cited` until this exists, which is honest but
incomplete. The LP bound is weak at small n, so the payoff is uncertain.

## Proposal

The Delsarte LP for GF(4) (Hamming scheme) and Z4 (Lee), solved with exact
rational dual certificates that a checker verifies over ℚ.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least one codetables.de upper bound is re-proved with a dual certificate the checker accepts.
