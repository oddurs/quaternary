## Ships

All four cells. Exact distance with certificates for GF(4)-linear codes, qubit
stabilizer codes, Z4-linear codes and ququart stabilizer codes, and the first
published table of ququart stabilizer codes.

## Done when

- [ ] `q4 distance` and `q4 check` work on one corpus code per cell, from the CLI and from Python.
- [ ] The ququart table is published as a ledger, every lower bound certified, every upper bound cited.

## Explicitly not in this milestone

- Nonexistence proofs: upper bounds stay cited theorems. Search beyond what the ququart table needs.
- The codetables.de audits (v0.4): the frontier is what needs them, and the maintainer's reply sets their scope.
