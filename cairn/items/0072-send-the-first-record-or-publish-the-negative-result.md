## Who

The maintainer of whichever table the baseline search improved (Aydin for Z4,
Grassl for GF(4) and qubit).

## What we ask or offer

A record report from `q4 submit`. If the search found nothing, publish a short
note giving the families, ranges and compute budget searched: negative results
save the next searcher's time.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A record report is sent, or the negative-result note is published, and its link recorded in a note.
